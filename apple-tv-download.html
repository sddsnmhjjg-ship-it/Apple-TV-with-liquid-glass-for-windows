How the Glass Works

Designed to Look and Feel Like Real Glass

Most apps just use a flat blurry box. Our Liquid Glass engine bends light, creates rainbow glints, and shapes realistic glass edges that react to your screen.

Real Light Bending

Light curves naturally around the rounded glass borders, giving you the real magnifying effect of curved optical glass.

Natural Light Physics

Rainbow Color Glints

Simulates tiny prism reflections along the glass bevels, giving your UI rich, subtle spectral colors that pop.

Prism Color Highlights

Crisp Border Shine

Adds bright white glass highlights along the top edge, catching the virtual room light for a premium clean shine.
